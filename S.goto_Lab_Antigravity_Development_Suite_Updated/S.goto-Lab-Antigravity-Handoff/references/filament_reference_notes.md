# Founder-Supplied Filament Sketch — Interpretation Notes

See `user_filament_sketch.png` (user-provided image, included for historical design context) and **`filament_corrected_geometry.svg`** (explicit corrected one-outgoing-line concept; not final artwork).

**Important correction:** The sketch depicts multiple grey branches continuing to the right of a central dot. The founder explicitly rejected that. The **approved conceptual geometry** is:

```
  incoming ────╮
  incoming ────┼─── [ ONE convergence node ] ═══════════▶ ONE outgoing line
  incoming ────╯
```

Actual curves may be graceful Bezier trajectories with 3–7 incoming fine strands; exactly one resolved outgoing path after the node. The dot and resolved line use the approved vermilion accent. There must be no additional grey/blue paths beyond the junction. Drawing is a **concept reference**, not a final site asset or permission to reproduce copyrighted timeline artwork.

## Behavior priorities

The animation gradually reveals the lines, consolidates them into one clear point, and follows the one continuing vermilion path. Provide static, no-JS, reduced-motion and narrow mobile variants.
